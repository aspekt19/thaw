How a check works

To begin, you paste the exchange notice, the amount, the date, and the deposit address or transaction id. TRON, Ethereum, and Arbitrum only.

Then you pay at least $1 in USDC, straight to our wallet on Base. The invoice amount is unique. A round dollar will not match.

After the chain confirms it, rules read the notice and the public transfer. You get a chance level, the reasons, and what would raise the chance. That $1 is credited if you open a file on the same check.

We decline sanctions, a police or court hold, theft, and a final closure. This is document preparation, not legal advice, and not a promise the withdrawal will be released.

Questions

What do you actually prepare?

A source-of-funds file for a regular user whose exchange froze an account or a withdrawal after an AML check. The exchange still makes the decision.

Will this release the withdrawal?

No. We do not promise that. Anyone who sells a guaranteed release is not us.

What does the dollar buy?

A chance level from the notice and the public chain data, plus the reasons. The fee is credited if you later open a light or full file on that same check.

Which cases do you refuse?

Sanctions, a police or court hold, theft, and a notice that the account is finally closed. A low chance also does not open a file.

Do you want my keys?

No. Never send a private key or a seed phrase. Payment is a normal USDC transfer from your own wallet to the address on the invoice.

Is this legal advice?

No. It is document preparation. A police or court hold needs a lawyer. Our papers do not replace one.

The file is paper, not a ruling

You keep your wallet. We receive one USDC transfer for the check, and a second for the remainder if you open a file. Both are verified on Base. Neither one gives us your keys.

Draft privacy

This page is a draft placeholder. It is not a finished privacy notice.

The check form collects the exchange, the notice text, an amount, an asset, a network, a deposit address or transaction id, a date, and where you say the funds came from. We store that with the invoice so we can run the check after payment and credit the fee.

Do not put a private key or a seed phrase in the notice. We reject fields that ask us to take one.

Payment is a public USDC transfer on Base. The transaction hash is stored so the same transfer cannot be reused.

A fuller notice has to name the operator, the retention period, and who can ask for a copy. Those are not settled in this draft.
