How a file is built

  1. Pay the check From $1 in USDC on Base. That payment is credited toward the file.
  2. Trace the deposit We read public history on TRON, Ethereum, Arbitrum, or Base. One page, not the whole chain.
  3. Read the notice Rules look for sanctions, a police or court hold, theft, or an ordinary source-of-funds check.
  4. Chance verdict High, medium, low, or declined, with the reasons. We do not promise a release.
  5. Case papers On a high or medium chance, or a low chance after you confirm the warnings, the check is credited toward a $49 or $79 file.

Draft terms

This page is a draft placeholder. It is not a finished contract.

Thaw prepares documents. It is not a law firm and not legal advice. We do not promise that an exchange will release a withdrawal or reopen an account.

The chance check is a paid read of your notice and of public chain data. The published minimum is $1, paid in USDC to the address on your invoice. That fee is credited toward a light file at $49 or a full file at $79 on the same check. The remainder is a second USDC transfer. A case is paid only after we verify that transfer.

We decline sanctions cases, police or court holds, theft, and final closures. We also can't take HTX-related cases for UK or EU residents. That is not legal advice. A low chance opens a Full file only after you confirm the warnings. We do not create, alter, or falsify any document.

You pay from your own wallet. We never ask for a private key or a seed phrase.

Draft privacy

This page is a draft placeholder. It is not a finished privacy notice.

The check form collects the exchange, the notice text, an amount, an asset, a network, a deposit address or transaction id, a date, and where you say the funds came from. We store that with the invoice so we can run the check after payment and credit the fee.

Do not put a private key or a seed phrase in the notice. We reject fields that ask us to take one.

Payment is a public USDC transfer on Base. The transaction hash is stored so the same transfer cannot be reused.

A fuller notice has to name the operator, the retention period, and who can ask for a copy. Those are not settled in this draft.
